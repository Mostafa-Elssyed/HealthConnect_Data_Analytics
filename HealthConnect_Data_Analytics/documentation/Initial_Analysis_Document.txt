1. DATASET OVERVIEW & STRUCTURAL AUDIT (Task 1 & 3)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The primary foundation for this investigation is built upon the source file 
"HealthConnect_Appointment_Data.csv" containing exactly 5,000 anonymized records. 
This data will be interpreted using the schema parameters defined within the 
"HealthConnect_Data_Dictionary.xlsx" reference matrix.

The dataset features are structurally classified into five investigative dimensions:
* Demographics: patient_id, gender, age, and age_group.
* Spatial Metrics: distance_to_clinic_km tracking geographic proximity barriers.
* Temporal / Scheduling Data: booking_lead_days, booking_date, and appointment_date.
* Clinic Logistical Metrics: waiting_time_minutes logged inside the facility.
* Historical Behavior: previous_appointments, previous_no_shows, reminder_sent,
  and reminder_channel.

3. TARGET BUSINESS QUESTIONS, METRIC SCHEMAS, AND KPI DESIGN (Task 4 & 5)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
To convert raw diagnostic data into actionable operational workflows for clinic 
management, the analysis will address five fundamental business concerns:

BUSINESS QUESTION 1: Operational Communication Effectiveness
* Question: Do automated digital reminders directly alter final patient attendance actions?
* Target KPI: Reminder Success Differential (RSD)
* Metric Formula: [Show Rate of Notified Cohort] - [Show Rate of Unnotified Cohort]
* Note: Show Rate is calculated exclusively using Net Appointments (Attended / (Attended + No-Show)).
* Actionable Application: Measures the precise conversion yield of communication channels.

BUSINESS QUESTION 2: Clinic Access Logistics & Service Barriers
* Question: Does excessive in-clinic wait time create operational friction that 
  deters future patient visits?
* Target KPI: Average Internal Wait Duration (AIWD)
* Metric Formula: Total Logged Minutes of Clinic Wait Time / Count of Net Appointments
* Actionable Application: Segments patient groups by wait times to isolate 
  the friction point where clinic delays trigger an increase in future no-shows.

BUSINESS QUESTION 3: Temporal Planning and Scheduling Friction
* Question: Does booking an appointment far in advance increase the risk of a 
  no-show due to forgetfulness?
* Target KPI: Lead-Time Attrition Index (LTAI)
* Metric Formula: (Count of No-Shows within Lead Category X / Total Net Bookings within Lead Category X) * 100
* Actionable Application: Tracks attendance windows to establish booking policies 
  that minimize long-range scheduling gaps.

BUSINESS QUESTION 4: Geographic Proximity and Location Constraints
* Question: Is physical distance from the facility a primary barrier to appointment compliance?
* Target KPI: Distance Variance No-Show Rate (DVNR)
* Metric Formula: (No-Shows within Distance Band Y / Total Net Bookings within Distance Band Y) * 100
* Actionable Application: Identifies geographic breaking points where the clinic 
  should deploy mobile health assets or expand digital telehealth access.

BUSINESS QUESTION 5: Chronic Behavioral Patterns & Patient History
* Question: Are missed appointments driven by the general population or by a small 
  group of repeat offenders?
* Target KPI: Repeat Attrition Concentration Ratio (RACR)
* Metric Formula: (No-Shows from Patients with previous_no_shows >= 1 / Total Global No-Shows) * 100
* Actionable Application: Flags chronic no-show patterns, allowing the clinic to 
  deploy manual confirmation calls for high-risk accounts.

4. STATISTICAL ASSUMPTIONS, CONSTRAINTS, & PROJECT RISKS (Part 3)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Data Assumption: Missing notification markers indicate that a system reminder 
  was never generated or sent, rather than indicating data corruption.
* Data Limitation: The anonymized dataset lacks external context (such as 
  seasonal weather variations, traffic patterns, or public transit access) 
  needed to fully explain the distance variables.
* Project Risk Factors: Deleting incomplete rows will compromise dataset 
  proportions and artificially alter the baseline attendance metrics; rows must 
  be preserved and treated programmatically.
