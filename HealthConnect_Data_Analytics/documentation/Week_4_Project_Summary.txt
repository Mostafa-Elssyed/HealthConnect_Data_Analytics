1. PROJECT TRACK STATEMENT
As a Data Analytics Intern on the HealthConnect project, my primary mandate is to
investigate the operational bottlenecks caused by missed patient appointments. 
The core objective this week was to analyze the data architecture and design a 
structured roadmap to find out exactly why patients default on their scheduled care.

2. PROGRAMMATIC RESOURCES REVIEWED
* Data Repository Ingested: HealthConnect_Appointment_Data.csv (5,000 unique records)
* Schema Definitions Utilized: HealthConnect_Data Dictionary resource file

3. CRITICAL FOUNDATIONAL OBSERVATIONS
* High Structural Void: Data quality checks flagged data gaps across three major 
  dimensions—specifically within patient communication logs, localized clinic wait 
  duration variables, and travel distance records.
* Baseline Attendance Strain: Initial profiling revealed that a substantial volume 
  of scheduling slots result in either cancellations or patient no-shows. This 
  vulnerability severely impacts clinic revenue and staff allocation efficiency.
* Dataset Structural Void: Data profiling confirmed 1,366 missing rows in reminder_channel, 90 in
  distance_to_clinic_km, and 60 in waiting_time_minutes. Additionally, exactly 2,647 records display
  logistical anomalies where missed or cancelled visits contain positive wait-time metrics.


4. INVESTIGATIVE PATHWAY FOR WEEK 5
To provide actionable business recommendations to clinic leadership, next week's 
analytical pipeline will focus on three key operational goals:
* Data Preparation: Build a localized Python pipeline to clean missing data fields 
  without deleting rows or distorting the underlying dataset properties.
* Behavioral Stratification: Group long-range text alerts and physical travel bands 
  into clear categories to find where patient compliance drops off.
* Executive Dashboarding: Connect our cleaned data outputs directly to Power BI 
  to build clear charts that map out high-risk customer traits.

5. RISK AND DEPENDENCY LOG
* Analytical Skew Risk: Deleting rows with missing or blank metrics will reduce our 
  sample size and distort baseline attendance calculations. All gaps must be isolated 
  via code rather than being removed.
* Contextual Limitations: Anonymized healthcare logs lack geographic or seasonal 
  context (like public transit route disruptions), meaning travel distance variations 
  must be interpreted with caution.
